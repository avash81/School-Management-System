# SchoolOS — User Research Recruitment and Consent Pack

**Status:** Draft templates for internal review only. They are not legal advice, a formal ethics approval, or evidence that participant consent has been obtained. Obtain school authorization and review by the responsible privacy/legal owner before use.

## Research purpose

Invite school staff and, where appropriate, students or guardians to help us understand school tasks and evaluate non-production SchoolOS workflow prototypes. Initial sessions focus on manual marks entry, AI-assisted marksheet review, approvals, permission-gated policy settings, and access to published results.

Research is about improving the workflow, not evaluating a participant's job performance, teaching, academic ability, or a student's marks.

We are seeking volunteers in these roles: [teacher/marks operator], [exam coordinator/administrator], and [principal/authorized approver]. Would your school be willing to review the study outline and, if approved, share this invitation with eligible volunteers? Please do not send us student lists, contact details, marks, or other personal records.

Thank you,
[research lead / project contact]

## School authorization checklist

Before recruitment or sessions begin, the project/research owner records:

- [ ] School's authorized contact has approved the proposed activity and recruitment route.
- [ ] School has confirmed whether its own research, safeguarding, or ethics process applies.
- [ ] Participant roles, session method, duration, and responsible facilitators are defined.
- [ ] Prototype and examples contain synthetic or properly anonymized data only.
- [ ] No production account, student record, live marksheet, credential, or confidential document will be used.
- [ ] Note-taking method, access controls, storage location, retention/deletion date, and responsible owner are approved.
- [ ] Recording is off by default. If proposed, its purpose, storage, access, retention, and separate opt-in are reviewed first.
- [ ] Participants are told participation is voluntary and unrelated to grades, employment decisions, or school services.
- [ ] A contact is available for questions, concerns, or withdrawal requests.
- [ ] Any child participation has separate school authorization, guardian permission, and age-appropriate child assent before the session.

Do not begin sessions until required items are completed and the responsible owner has approved the study materials.

## Adult participant consent script

Read before the session and provide a copy to the participant:

> We are asking for your feedback on SchoolOS workflows. We want to understand the design, not evaluate you. You will try a non-production prototype with fictional or anonymized examples. Please do not provide real student or staff information, passwords, or confidential school documents.
>
> Taking part is voluntary. You may skip a question, pause, or stop at any time without penalty. We plan to take notes about workflow feedback using a participant code and role, not your name in the research notes. [Describe approved storage, who can see notes, and deletion date.] We will not audio/video record this session unless you separately opt in; declining recording will not affect participation.
>
> Do you understand the information, have a chance to ask questions, and agree to take part under these conditions?

Record only the participant code, role, date, consent method, and yes/no decision in the approved consent log. Do not put signatures or contact details in research notes unless the approved process requires them and specifies protected storage.

## Student and guardian participation

Do not invite or run sessions with minors until the school and responsible privacy/safeguarding owner approve the activity and confirm the applicable permission process.

Use a separate, age-appropriate explanation for the student. Obtain guardian permission and the student's affirmative assent before participation; a guardian's permission does not replace the child's assent. If either does not agree, do not proceed. A student may stop or skip any activity at any point without penalty. Do not ask about grades, personal circumstances, or actual marks.

Do not audio/video record minors in the initial research. If recording is later proposed, require an explicit, separate review and opt-in from the guardian and the child, plus school approval; otherwise do not record.

### Guardian permission language — draft

> Your child is invited to try a non-production SchoolOS prototype and tell us whether the instructions and screens are understandable. The activity is not a test, will not affect grades or school services, and uses fictional examples only. Your child does not need to answer any question and may stop at any time. We will not ask for real student records or marks. Notes will use a participant code and role; [insert the approved storage/access/deletion details].
>
> Do you give permission for your child to take part under these conditions? You may withdraw permission by contacting [responsible contact].

### Student assent language — draft

> We are trying out some SchoolOS screens to see whether they make sense. This is not a test of you. The examples are made up. You can skip anything or stop whenever you want. Your choice will not affect your grades or school services. Is it okay with you to take part?

## Session close

At the end, remind participants:

- not to share screenshots or prototype links containing internal material outside the approved group;
- how to contact the research owner with questions or a withdrawal request;
- that prototype feedback does not mean the feature is built, approved, or available in SchoolOS.
